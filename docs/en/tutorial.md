# Online Flight Tutorial

Welcome to XFLYSIM online flight! This tutorial will help you complete your first online flight.

## Getting Started

### 1. Register an account and callsign

- Register an account on the online flight website and apply for a valid callsign.
- Your callsign is your unique identifier once connected to the server. Keep it safe, and **do not lend your account to others**.

### 2. Prepare your software

You will need the following software:

| Purpose | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Flight simulator | Such as Microsoft Flight Simulator, X-Plane, etc. |
| Online flight client | Used to connect to the XFLYSIM online flight server |
| XVoice | Voice communication software, required for voice crews connecting to ATC |
| Online flight map | Used to check whether controllers are online in the current flight airspace |

### 3. Connect to the server

- Server address: `ol.xflysim.com`
- **Connect at the fixed parking stands designated by the airport.** Connecting directly on taxiways, runways or other non-stand positions is strictly prohibited.
- After connecting to the server, **submit a valid flight plan as soon as possible** (including departure/arrival airports, cruise flight level, planned route, aircraft type, etc.).

### 4. Communication frequency

- When flying online without a controller, please use the **public frequency 122.800**.
- When a controller comes online, they will make a text call on the public channel (`Please contact me on XXX.XX`). At that time, tune the communications panel to the controller's frequency to establish contact.
- **Text-based crews**: communicate directly via text within the game.
- **Voice crews**: connect the XVoice voice software.

### 5. Fly

Within controlled airspace, follow controller instructions, observe the "left-window yield principle", and carefully read the [User Guidelines](/en/rules) before flying.

## FAQ

### How should the transponder be set?

Set the transponder to Mode C from entering the takeoff runway until vacating the landing runway, and to Mode S at all other times. Use IDENT mode only when the controller requests identification.

### What is AFK flight?

A state in which you are connected to the server but unable to respond to controller or supervisor instructions, such as going out for meals or sleeping. AFK flight is strictly prohibited during activities.

### How should emergency codes be used?

The use of codes 7500 and 7600 is prohibited on online flight. If you genuinely have a fault, you may squawk 7700; emergency codes must not be squawked under normal circumstances.
