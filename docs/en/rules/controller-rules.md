# XFLYSIM Controller Online Flight Regulations

> These regulations are implemented as a companion to the [XFLYSIM User Guidelines](/en/rules/user-rules), and shall be followed by controllers when providing air traffic control services (hereinafter "control services"). These regulations take effect at 00:00 Beijing Time on 1 November 2022.

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

**Article 1** These regulations are formulated to regulate the conduct of controllers providing control services on the XFLYSIM online flight server, to maintain the order of online flight (hereinafter "online flight"), in accordance with the XFLYSIM User Guidelines (hereinafter "the Guidelines") and the actual practice of online flight.

**Article 2** These regulations apply to all controllers who apply for and open control positions and provide control services on the XFLYSIM online flight server (`ol.xflysim.com`).

**Article 3** When providing services, controllers shall comply with national laws and regulations, the currently effective aeronautical information (AIP/NAIP) and all rules of this platform; where flight levels are concerned, the [RVSM metric flight level allocation rules in China](/en/knowledge/rvsm) shall be followed.

**Article 4** Terms used in these regulations are defined as follows:

- **Controller**: a member who is recognized by the platform with the appropriate rating, connects to the server through the online flight software, and provides air traffic control services over a defined area.
- **Control position**: a set of frequencies and the corresponding airspace (e.g. tower, approach, area control) used by a controller to provide services.
- **Controlled airspace, event airspace and event period**: the definitions are consistent with the Guidelines.

## Chapter 2 Rating and Position Application

**Article 5** A controller shall obtain a control rating recognized by the platform and complete the corresponding training and examination before applying to open a control position.

**Article 6** Before opening a position, the controller shall publish an opening announcement in advance on the online flight website or the event post, stating the airspace, frequency, service period and controller callsign, and open the corresponding position on the online flight map.

**Article 7** The same controller shall not open multiple control positions at the same time; multiple positions genuinely required for operational needs shall be approved by the platform's ATC center.

**Article 8** A controller shall connect to the server with their own registered callsign, fill in the callsign and rating identifier according to platform regulations, and shall not use another person's callsign.

## Chapter 3 Control Service Standards

**Article 9** After opening the position, the controller shall remain on duty on the announced frequency; if temporarily away, an announcement shall be made and the service suspended, and no aircraft shall be accepted again until service resumes.

**Article 10** Clearances and instructions issued by controllers shall be clear and unambiguous, with standard phraseology and consistent with the Guidelines; correct flight levels shall be used and kept consistent with the pilots' flight plans.

**Article 11** Voice control shall use standard phraseology (Chinese or English), following the requirements of clear instruction and read-back; text control shall provide an equivalent service through the platform's text channel, ensuring instructions can be acknowledged by pilots.

**Article 12** Controllers shall implement sequencing and separation according to the SID/STAR, routes and flight levels published in the currently effective AIP, and shall not alter procedures at will; where deviation is genuinely necessary, confirmation with the pilot and an explanation are required.

**Article 13** Controllers shall adopt clearance, radar vectoring and separation measures according to traffic load to ensure conflict-free operations; during events, priority shall be given to event traffic.

**Article 14** When handing off between adjacent positions, coordination on altitude, speed and aircraft status shall be completed, and the handoff point and next frequency shall be stated on the frequency.

**Article 15** Controllers shall use polite language and shall not insult or harass pilots or other controllers; when a pilot violation is observed, it shall be recorded and reported following the process in the Guidelines, and no unauthorized punishment shall be imposed.

## Chapter 4 Opening, Closing and Duty

**Article 16** When starting (opening) or ending (closing) control services, the controller shall publish an announcement and open or close the corresponding position on the online flight map.

**Article 17** Before closing, the controller shall ensure that all aircraft in the controlled area have been handed off or their service has ended, with no remaining conflicts.

**Article 18** If unable to continue providing service due to network failure or other special circumstances, the controller shall immediately notify the adjacent controller or the platform and assist in completing the handoff, and shall not go offline without notice.

## Chapter 5 Control During Events

**Article 19** During online flight events, control positions shall be opened according to the requirements of the event post, and all aircraft in the event airspace shall receive the corresponding control service.

**Article 20** Clearance, pushback, taxi and takeoff clearances during events shall be issued in an orderly manner according to the event arrangements, with priority given to event route traffic.

**Article 21** Controllers shall assist platform supervisors in maintaining event order; for behaviors such as disobeying instructions or AFK flying, record and report them according to the process in the Guidelines.

## Chapter 6 Violation Handling

**Article 22** Where a controller commits any of the following, the platform may, depending on the circumstances, impose a verbal warning, suspension of the position, or revocation of the control rating:

1. providing control services not matching their rating or beyond the airspace scope;
2. leaving the position unattended for a long time, closing without authorization, or leaving conflicts at closing;
3. issuing instructions contrary to the aeronautical information or clearly erroneous;
4. quarreling with pilots or other controllers or using inappropriate language;
5. opening multiple positions without approval or using another person's callsign;
6. other acts violating these regulations or the Guidelines.

**Article 23** The result of handling against a controller shall be published on the platform; where circumstances are serious or major impact is caused, the case shall be referred to the platform's ATC center.

## Chapter 7 Supplementary Provisions

**Article 24** Matters not covered by these regulations shall be executed by the platform in accordance with the Guidelines and the currently effective aeronautical information.

**Article 25** These regulations are interpreted by the XFLYSIM online flight platform and shall take effect on the date of publication.
